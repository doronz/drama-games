How a session works

An hour of games, role-play, and reflection

Kids never sit through a lecture. They move, laugh, and act, and the skills grow inside the play.

1

Warm-up games

Movement and drama games get everyone listening, laughing, and taking turns.

2

Today's topic

A short talk, often with a picture book or video, about the day's skill.

3

Act it out

Kids role-play real situations and try different ways to handle them.

4

Closing circle

We review the new skills we learned, so everyone leaves proud of their progress.

Example letter · Challenges in Friendships

Dear Parents,

This week we explored the challenges that can come up in friendships, and practiced how to handle them:

  • "I wasn't invited to the birthday party."It's okay to feel sad. Not being invited doesn't mean nobody likes me.
  • "My friend is playing with someone else."We practiced flexible thinking: we can ask, "Can I play too?" or find another way to play.
  • "My friend is too bossy."We practiced being assertive: "I want a turn to choose."

As always, we played drama games, watched a short video, and used movement to explore these ideas.

Best, Eti

Questions

Parents often ask

Is this right for a shy child?

Yes. The games start small and build slowly, and no one is pushed into the spotlight before they're ready. Quiet kids often end up loving the scenes most.

Does my child need acting experience?

No. This isn't a theater class. Drama is just the tool, and every game is designed for kids who have never acted before.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a social-emotional skills group led by an experienced SEL teacher, for any child who wants to grow in confidence and friendship. If your child already works with a therapist, the group fits in well alongside that.

What if my child misses a session?

There are no make-up sessions, but you'll get Eti's letter for that day, so you can go over what the group practiced at home.

What is your refund policy?

You get a full refund if you cancel before the first session.

Where do the groups meet?

At Eti's home in Sunnyvale. You'll receive the address after you sign up.
